Brand Story

Why Malik Musk exists

Malik Musk was born from a simple belief: that real power doesn't announce itself — it's felt. Each bottle carries a woody, musky signature designed for those who lead quietly and never chase the room.

Visual Identity

Palette & positioning

Positioning — Accessible luxury. Malik Musk sits between everyday wear and true prestige fragrance: premium and personal, without an outrageous price tag.

Logo — A minimalist wordmark in a clean, slightly bold serif, with a subtle crown worked into the letter M — a quiet nod to "Malik" meaning king.

Bottle Design

Architectural, matte, unmistakable

A squared-off, heavy-base glass bottle with an architectural silhouette. Matte black glass, a gold cap, and a minimal centered wordmark — nothing cluttered. It should feel like it belongs on a shelf, not hidden in a drawer.

Behind the Brand

Built on the same idea it's named for

Malik Musk isn't a boardroom concept — it's a personal one. The scent, the name, the quiet confidence behind it all trace back to a single founder who wanted a fragrance that says something without saying much at all.

— Malik
Campaign Scope

Three phases to launch

Phase I · Weeks 1–2

Teaser

Moody, close-up shots of the bottle in shadow. No full reveal — captions hint at "quiet power" without showing the product.

Phase II

Reveal

Full product photography and the brand story go live, alongside honest first impressions from fragrance-focused micro-influencers on Reels and TikTok.

Phase III

Sustain

Ongoing content — real people wearing it, testimonials, and a limited-time code for early buyers to convert buzz into sales.
